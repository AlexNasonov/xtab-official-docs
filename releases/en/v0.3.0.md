**Improvements**
- New field types: email, URL, phone number, currency, percent, duration, rating, multiple select, user (one or several people).
- Links between tables: linked records as pills, picking from a list with search, a field back in the linked table, pasting links by name, and filtering, sorting and grouping by a link.
- Lookup, count and rollup with a condition on the linked records; their values update by themselves when the linked records change.
- Formulas: more than a hundred functions — arithmetic, conditions, text, dates in the time zone of the organization with business days, lists, regular expressions.
  - Suggestions of fields and functions as you type, mistakes shown in place, a description of the arguments of a function, the result on the selected record before saving.
  - A recount on every change, in the background in big tables.
  - Filters, sorting, grouping and summaries by the result of a formula; a formula can be the primary field.
- The primary field of a table can be chosen in the field menu.
- Attachments: files in cells, added with a button or by dropping them, image thumbnails right in the table, and PDFs viewed in a window of xtab.
  - An uploaded file's type is checked by its content.
  - Photos lose the information about the place, the camera and the author.
  - A file removed from a cell comes back with undo.
- Password fields have a button that shows the typed password.
- A grouped view shows its first records before the headers of its groups.

**Fixes**
- A new link could keep a stale lookup and sort by the old name when the linked record was changed at the same moment.
- A background job on a deleted table no longer retries until it runs out of attempts.
