**Improvements**
- Organization, workspaces, bases and tables; the roles «owner», «creator», «editor», «commenter» and «reader» in workspaces and bases.
- Login with a password, accepting an invitation and resetting a password by e-mail; invitations and roles are given through the API.
- A grid: scrolling through any number of records, a pinned primary field, coloured choices, checkboxes, a «New record» row, the number of records at the bottom.
- Editing cells with the keyboard and the mouse; numbers with spaces and a decimal comma.
- Fields: a «+» column for a new field, a header menu to change, insert on the left or right, delete.
- A whole record: every field of one record, the arrows move to the neighbours.
- Real-time collaboration: edits, new and deleted records and changes of fields are visible to everyone at once.
- A home page with workspaces and base tiles, table tabs, jump anywhere with Ctrl+K, light and dark themes.
- REST API `/api/v1` with operations on the structure and an OpenAPI specification.
- Delivery: an image and offline bundles for linux/amd64 and linux/arm64 with signatures, Docker Compose with PostgreSQL, SeaweedFS and Caddy.

**Fixes**
- The first version: no fixes over earlier releases.
