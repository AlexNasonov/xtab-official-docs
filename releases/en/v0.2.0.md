**Improvements**
- Several views of a table: a panel of views on the left, hidden fields, column widths and row height in each view.
- A filter with conditions and condition groups, sorting by several fields — buttons above the table light up and show the number of conditions. Without the right to change a view, a filter and a sort can be set for yourself only.
- Grouping by several fields with collapsible groups, a summary row under the columns (sum, average, minimum, maximum and more), record colours by a field or by conditions.
- A changed record stays in place until you leave the row, even if the filter or the sort would put it elsewhere.
- Search in a view (Ctrl+F) and search for records in the quick jump (Ctrl+K).
- Selecting a range of cells, copying and pasting from Excel and Google Sheets and back, fill down, clearing and deleting the selected records.
- Undo and redo (Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z), a new record below the selected one (Shift+Enter).
- Import from CSV and Excel: a new table from a file or records into an existing table, choice of fields and types, a report of the values that did not fit, deleting the imported records within a day.
- Export of a view to CSV and Excel with its filter and sort.
- English interface, e-mails and server messages; the language is chosen in the account, numbers and dates follow the language.
- The user guide in Russian and English.

**Fixes**
- When the server stops or restarts, open pages get a proper close of the connection instead of a broken one.
