The second candidate of version 0.9: fixes after the check of a restore on the pilot stand.

**Improvements**
- The fragment of the Prometheus settings in the bundle scrapes the service `xtab-sealer` too: the alerts of the seals of the audit log fire.
- `restore.sh` takes the backup key by its full path — from a separate medium, for example (`--key /media/keys/backup_key`).

**Fixes**
- A restore from a backup (`restore.sh`) on an installation made by the installer stopped at its first step: the node could not read the backup key.
- `xtab doctor` on the interface node reported the default port and protection of the mail server instead of the configured ones.
