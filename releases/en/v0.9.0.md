The ninth version — improvements after the pilot: forms, lists, columns and sorting in interfaces, a condition of choosing a link, a manual order of records, several triggers and a trial run of automations, a number format for formulas, a safe rollback of an update, and fixes after the remarks of the pilot and the security reviews. In detail — in the notes of the candidates 0.9.0-rc.1…rc.6.

**Improvements**
- Interfaces: a Form element — a new record, a change of the chosen one or a view of it, with required fields, labels, hints and conditions of showing; a form of a new record keeps to the filters of its element; the «Open a form» and «Delete the record» buttons. Record fields saved earlier work as a form of a new record.
- Interfaces: a look of its own for the List, columns on a page, sorting, a manual order of records and column widths in a Grid and a List.
- The interface builder: a preview on the real data, a draft that saves itself, the order of pages and fields by dragging and from the keyboard, the editor of a form with fields dragged into place, the name and the description of an interface right in the builder.
- Link fields: a condition of choosing records (for example, only the Budgets of the chosen Project) and the name of the inverse field when the link is created; grouping by a link in the order of the records of the linked table.
- A manual order of records in a table without a sort — by dragging and with the keys; one order of the tabs of tables and of the views for everyone; a table opens in the view last chosen in it, a base — in the table last opened; an «All tables» menu and tabs hidden for oneself.
- A new record in a filtered view, a kanban, an interface or a form takes the values of the filter at once.
- Picking linked records, people and choices everywhere is a compact field with a search and a choice from the keyboard; choices of selects have colours, are reordered and are pasted as a list.
- Formulas, lookups and rollups of numbers: a format — Number, Currency or Percent; the «Show the currency sign» and «Show the time» checkboxes.
- Automations: up to five triggers, «Try on a record» — a trial run before turning on, values of the answer of an HTTP request in the next steps, duplicating, a run log with the reasons of failures in words.
- The grid: menus by the right button and from the keyboard (Shift+F10), a «New record» button, faster drawing.
- Workspaces, bases and tables are renamed and deleted right in the interface; the actions are gathered in the «Manage base» and «Manage workspace» menus.
- An announcement of the administrator of the installation — a banner for every user, the sign-in page included.
- Sign-in and sessions: no more than 10 sessions at once, the second factor locks after 10 wrong codes in a row, a password may not hold the name of the product, of the organization or one's own name; a sign-in through a provider counts as the second factor only when this is on in the settings of the provider and the provider confirmed it; protection of the interface against injected scripts.
- The signing of the audit log runs in a service of its own, backups and the archive of the audit are signed by a key of their own; the metrics of the nodes are served only in a network of their own for monitoring.
- Accessibility: unavailable buttons stay reachable from the keyboard and tell why, outcomes of actions are announced to screen readers; pages fit a narrow window and a phone.

**Fixes**
- Formulas reading one another in a «diamond» (many layers of formulas over one field) slowed an edit of the source field down to tens of seconds and could take up the whole server — a way to a denial of service; now the check of formulas on a write does not depend on how they are linked.
- A secret of an «HTTP request» step the external service sent back in its answer could partly reach the run log and a trial run; now it is cut out whole. Addresses of the recipients of emails are now hidden in the logs of the server and of the runs.
- When the policy of the organization asks everyone for a second factor, a session without it no longer gets attachments and the list of interfaces — only the profile works until the second factor is set up.
- Rollback after an update that changes the database: the previous version comes back with `--rollback` unless the update changed the database in a way it does not take; otherwise the installer restores the copy taken before the update by itself.
- A number of a formula or a rollup lost its fraction in an export, on a change of the field type and in the name of a linked record; stored settings of a field or a view that could not be read were silently wiped; renaming a choice of a select erased it in every record.
- The grid: one's own edited or new record jumped away or disappeared in a table with grouping, sorting or a filter; a count of the records of a large table with a filter failed.
- The interface builder: an interface was not saved in some cases (a deleted field, a deleted filter, a card over another table); the «Create a record» button created the record in the wrong table.
- A public form with a file could be «uploading» without end; dates in the run log, of members, API tokens and passkeys were shown in UTC.
- A restore from a backup on an installation made by the installer stopped at its first step; backups never left the spool when files are kept in the customer's S3.
- Security: a deleted row of the license removed every limit after a restart, the check of the audit log accepted unsigned checkpoints, an expired password was changed without the second factor, an import of an Excel file with two distant cells took gigabytes of memory; the mail library and the library that parses web addresses are updated with fixes of vulnerabilities.

**Before updating**
- From 0.9.0-rc.6: the database does not change, and `sudo ./install.sh --rollback` brings rc.6 back.
- From 0.8.0: the update changes the database, and 0.8.0 does not work with it — `--rollback` cannot bring 0.8.0 back. The way back is the copy taken before the update (`sudo ./install.sh --restore`); changes after it are lost. If 0.9.0 fails to start during the update, the installer restores the copy by itself.
- From 0.8.0: the metrics of the nodes are now served only in the Docker network `monitoring` — attach Prometheus to it (the chapter «Monitoring» of the installation guide).
