A candidate of version 0.9 for the pilot: fixes after the acceptance and the internal security review.

**Improvements**
- The grid: the menus of a field and of cells open by the right button, sorting from the menu of the header of a field; a «New record» button over the grid; the list of choices opens by a second click on the cell; thinner and softer frames of the selection.
- Colours of the choices of select fields and a default for a field of several choices.
- The editor of a long text is pulled by its corner and expands into the window of its record.
- The grid draws faster: fewer full reads, its own edits are not applied twice, a new height of rows shows at once, rows show without waiting when you come back to a table.
- Workspaces, bases and tables are renamed and deleted right in the interface; the title of the browser tab names the base, the table and the view.
- An announcement of the administrator of the installation: a banner for every user, the login page included — about planned maintenance, say; everyone sees it within a minute.
- Mail: a probe of the mail server at installation; a new password of the mail is picked up without a restart.
- Login and sessions: no more than 10 sessions of an account at once (set in the password policy), a new session id after an action is confirmed, recovery codes kept under a key of the installation, the second factor locks after 10 wrong codes in a row, a password may not hold the name of the product, of the organization or one's own name.
- A login through a provider counts as the second factor of an administrator only when the provider setting «The provider confirms a second factor» is on and the provider said so at the login.
- Protection of the interface against injected scripts (Trusted Types); requests to the API over plain HTTP are refused.
- The signing of the audit log moved to a service of its own, `xtab-sealer`; backups and the archive of the audit are signed by a key of their own, `secrets/files_signing_key` (the installer makes it on update), and the signature of a backup covers the name of its file.
- The metrics of the nodes are served only in a Docker network of their own, `monitoring`: attach Prometheus to it (the chapter «Monitoring» of the installation guide).

**Fixes**
- Installation with mail: the node of the interface did not start without the address of the sender.
- «Insert a field left/right» added the column at the end of the view.
- The form of a field did not change the settings of a link, a lookup or a rollup.
- An import of an Excel file with two distant cells took gigabytes of memory.
- Backups never left the spool when files are kept in the customer's S3.
- A deleted row of the license removed every limit after a restart.
- The check of the audit log accepted unsigned checkpoints.
- An expired password was changed without the second factor.
- The check of the installation marked a working installation as failing.
