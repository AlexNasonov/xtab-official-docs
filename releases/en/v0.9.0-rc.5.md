The fifth candidate of version 0.9 for the pilot: the Form, a look of its own for the List and columns in interfaces, a condition of choosing a link, a manual order of records, and fixes.

**Improvements**
- Interfaces: a Form element instead of Record fields — a new record, a change of the chosen one or a view of it; fields required or read-only, labels, hints and conditions of showing of their own; the form on the page or «Only in a window». A record of a Grid or a Kanban opens in the form, «+» opens the form of a new record; Record fields saved earlier work as a form of a new record.
- The «Open a form» and «Delete the record» buttons of an interface — a deletion asks for a confirmation and is there only where the element may delete.
- Interfaces: the List element has a look of its own — records as rows with the main field large and up to four fields under it, grouping by a field, search and the number of records; a record is chosen by a click or from the keyboard for the card, the form and the buttons of the page. Lists saved earlier show the new look at once; records are no longer changed in the rows of a list — use a Form or a Record card to change them.
- Interface pages: columns — a row of 2 or 3 columns, each with its elements one under another at a height of its own (say, a grid on the left, a card and buttons on the right); on a narrow screen the columns stand one under another. A new page «List and card» is laid out in columns.
- Link fields: a condition of choosing records in the settings of the field («Choose only records where…») narrows the list to choose from by the fields of the linked table, by links further along and by the value of a field of the record being filled in: for example, a Contract offers only the Budgets of the Project chosen in it. A link that no longer fits is not removed but marked.
- The condition works in the forms of views too — they can now ask a Link field (a public form asks no links) — and in the Form, Grid and Record card elements of an interface.
- Manual order of records: in a table without a sort, drag rows by their numbers, move them with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ or from the menu, and undo a move with Ctrl+Z; the menu has new items «Insert record above» and «Insert record below». The order is shared by every view of the table without a sort and other users see it at once; in a grouped table rows move within their group.
- The order of the tabs of tables and of the views: a creator of the base moves them by dragging or with Alt+← and Alt+→ (tabs), Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ (views); the order is the same for everyone.
- A new record in a filtered view, a kanban, an interface or the form of «+» takes the values of the conditions of the filter at once (say, «Status is In progress») and does not drop out of sight.
- Choices of a Single select and a Multiple select in «Edit field» and in the window of a new field: moved by buttons, by dragging and with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓, a pasted list is split into choices, each has a colour of its own; deleting a choice that records have asks for a confirmation with the number of those records. The window of a new field has the «Default and validation» block for every type.
- The grid: a search in the list of a Multiple select; «Fill down» copies links too.
- The user guide: a reference of every formula function by groups with examples.

**Fixes**
- Renaming a choice of a Single or Multiple select in «Edit field» erased it in every record.
- The grid: a letter typed on a cell of a link, a user or a Multiple select did not go into the search, and a second one typed quickly was lost; the name of a linked record with commas was shown as several records; «Fill down» over a Link field failed; a new record in a group by a link fell into the «(empty)» group; lists and menus of the bottom rows were cut by the edge of the table.
- A lookup of texts could not be chosen in a filter, a sort or a grouping; nothing told why Link fields and attachments cannot be looked up and how to get around it.
- The formula editor: the selected text of a formula became invisible, the suggestions of fields opened under the window of the field.
- The default value of a select could not be chosen before its choices were saved; a long name of a view pushed the buttons of the panel under the grid.
- The interface builder: an interface was not saved after deleting a Filter element another element was bound to (now the deletion removes such references and names the dependent elements); a filter condition on a deleted field was not shown; «Delete the record» and cancelling a new record were available without the «Delete» permission.
- The «Create a record» button created records in the first table of the base instead of the table of its element and did not warn that the record would be empty; Record fields with a Link field were not saved.

**Before updating**
- A rollback: once an interface with columns is saved or published after the update, it will not open on the previous version of the server until it is updated again; other interfaces keep working.
