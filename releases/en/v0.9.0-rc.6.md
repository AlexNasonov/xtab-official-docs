The sixth candidate of version 0.9 for the pilot: sorting and a manual order in the elements of interfaces, a form of a new record kept to the filters of its element, an order of pages and fields in the builder, several triggers of an automation, a number format for formulas, a rollback after an update that changes the database, and fixes after the remarks of the pilot.

**Improvements**
- Picking linked records, people and choices of a Multiple select everywhere — in a cell, the record window, forms, the elements of interfaces, filter conditions and «Try on a record» — is a compact field with a drop-down list: a search, a choice from the keyboard, more items loaded as you scroll.
- Cells of Link, User and Attachment fields show the total number of values at the right edge instead of «+N»; a record without a name is labelled «Untitled», a member without a name — «No name». In the record window an Attachment field has the same list of files as the cell: open, add, remove.
- Lookups: in the grid the values are shown as separate chips, in the record window — separated by «; ».
- A table opens in the view last chosen in it, a base — in the table last opened.
- Grouping by a Link field — the «In record order» direction: the groups stand as the records stand in the linked table; drag a record there and its group moves too. In the grid and in the List of an interface.
- Formulas, lookups and rollups of numbers: a format — Number, Currency or Percent, the decimal places and the currency; the summaries under the column and the example «On the selected record» show the same look. Number formulas, rollups and Count fields stand at the right edge of the grid, as numbers do.
- The Currency field and amounts in the Currency format: the «Show the currency sign» checkbox.
- The «Show the time» checkbox of date-and-time fields, of the created and modified time and of formulas, lookups and rollups giving a date: without it only the date is shown everywhere.
- Link fields: «Name of the inverse field» is set when the link is created; the window of the link shows the inverse field and has a «Go to the field» button.
- Interfaces, Grid and List: sorting — the creator sets up to three fields, a user sorts for oneself with the «Sort» button or by the header of a column, «Reset» brings back the order of the creator; the «Change the order» action — dragging records, «Move up / down» and inserting a new record above or below the chosen one.
- Interfaces, Grid: column widths are set by the creator (by the edge of the header or as a number) and changed by a user for oneself, a double click on the edge brings back the width of the creator; when no form to open is chosen, a new record is added as a row at the bottom of the grid and created by Enter or on leaving the row.
- A form of a new record from an element of an interface keeps to its filters: the values of the filter are filled in and marked «Set by the filter», the allowed choices are narrowed, a refusal names the filter and the allowed value; the builder warns about filters and a form that contradict each other.
- A form of a new record from an element shows the limits of the filter at once: choices the filter holds have no «×», the choice of people is narrowed, a link set by the filter is read-only, numbers and dates have bounds — a value outside them is refused before sending.
- The interface builder, the editor of a Form: the «Fields of the table» list with a search and «Add to the form», fields on the canvas are dragged and resized, the settings of a field open under it; the «Create an edit form» and «Create a new-record form» buttons.
- The builder: the order of pages — by dragging, with Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ or by the «Up» and «Down» buttons; the order of the fields of a Grid, a List, a Kanban and a Record card — in one table of fields with the shown ones on top, the primary field always first; the «Fields under it» of a List keep an order of their own. In the table of fields the «Up» and «Down» buttons show up when the pointer is over the row and are always seen on a touch screen.
- The builder: the palette in the row of the canvas, a new element stands under the chosen one; the name of an interface is edited right in the header, its description — in «Settings of the interface», the list of interfaces has «Rename the interface» and «Delete the interface»; a Text element understands headings (`#`, `##`) and lists (`-`); the buttons on the canvas and in a form are icons whose tooltip names the element or the field.
- Automations: up to five triggers of one automation («Another trigger»); the trigger that started a run is shown in the log and is available to the steps. «Duplicate» makes a turned-off copy «(copy)», «(copy 2)» and so on — without the values of secrets.
- The run log of automations: times in the format of the language and in the time zone of the browser, the reasons of failures in words of the language of the interface with the number of the step and the place; «Details» opens the steps under their own row.
- The editor of automations: long values, addresses and texts of emails are seen whole in fields of several lines; the «Field — Was — Will be» table of «Try on a record» stays readable with long values; deleting a secret asks for a confirmation.
- The grid: the menus of a cell, of a field and the panels open whole inside the window; Shift+F10 opens the menu of the cell, Ctrl+Shift+F10 — the menu of the field, the arrows go through the items, an unavailable item tells why.
- Unavailable buttons across the application stay reachable from the keyboard and tell why in their tooltip; unavailable checkboxes, lists and inputs look inactive; pressing a submit button again does not send twice.
- Tabs of tables have a limited width (a long name ends with an ellipsis, the whole one is in the tooltip), the buttons of the base are always seen and show as icons on a laptop; pages fit a narrow window and a phone, the panel of views opens over the grid in a narrow window.
- Deleting a sign-in provider or a SIEM receiver asks for a confirmation; the license file is chosen with a «Choose a file» button.

**Fixes**
- Rollback after an update that changes the database: the previous version starts again with `--rollback` unless the update changed the database in a way it does not take; otherwise the installer restores the copy taken before a failed update by itself and offers to restore it on `--rollback`.
- A number of a formula or a rollup lost its fraction in an export to CSV and Excel, on a change of the field type and in the name of a linked record; now a change of type rounds only to the decimal places of the new field, and the preview tells how many numbers will be rounded, with an example.
- Stored settings of a field or a view that could not be read were silently taken as empty — the field disappeared, the settings were wiped; now an error is shown and the settings are not written over.
- An open form view did not see changed questions until the page was reloaded; now the changes show up at once and the answers typed in are kept.
- The «Edit field» window closed when the structure of the table was changed in another window or by another user; now the window stays, and if its own field was changed it offers «Refresh» without silently losing what was typed. A successful change of the field type showed «The operation failed».
- The «Edit field» window of a lookup, a Count and a rollup failed with «this type of field has no such settings»; now the expression and the condition of a rollup change with a recount of the values.
- The value of a condition of choosing a link chosen last before «Save» was not saved; the list to choose from was too narrow in a narrow column, and long conditions stretched the window of the field; the «Go to the field» link broke into two lines; on a quick change of the link in the window of a new field the fields of the previous link could show up.
- Pasting names into a Link field: with many similar records the right one was not found, and a failed check was told as «no such record».
- Copying (Ctrl+C) numbers, percents, dates and checkboxes of formulas and rollups, values of lookups and attachments did not follow the language of the interface, and pasting read them wrongly; the values of a lookup of numbers ran together into one line.
- The grid: the height of rows was reset by a change of the filter or the sorting; in the field window and in the panels of sorting and grouping the focus was lost after saving, deleting and reordering.
- Changes by other people: a new formula field created together with a bulk change of records was seen by other tabs only after reloading the page; after a confirmation by password the updates came late; after the session ended the tab kept reconnecting instead of going to the sign-in.
- A public form with a file could be «uploading» without end; now getting ready to send is retried by itself, a failure has «Try again», and an overload tells «The server is busy, try again in a minute».
- The interface builder: the «Shown» and «May change» checkboxes disagreed with the settings; «Up» and «Down» did not move an element alone in its row; quick presses of Alt+↑ and Alt+↓ were lost; the line with the outcome of a reorder shifted the canvas and the settings of the page; a page with elements was deleted without asking; a refused save did not tell what to fill in; at a zoom of 150 % and in a narrow window the canvas and the settings did not fit; an autosave dropped the focus in the settings of an element.
- The editor of automations: Enter in the secrets and in the search of «Try on a record» saved the whole automation; a save with secrets not set gave no hint; the focus was lost on a change of the screen and after moving steps.
- Dates in the run log, of members, API tokens and passkeys were shown in UTC; the date of the license could shift by a day. The label of the «Time zone» field of the profile tells that dates are shown in the time zone of the browser for now.
- Removing an announcement of the administrator of the installation could miss the audit log on a failure.
- Accessibility: outcomes of actions were not announced to screen readers, the focus was lost in the forms of sign-in, of a confirmation by password and of the administration, the title of the browser tab did not name the shown screen; the hints inside inputs were hard to see in the dark theme, and the borders of inputs — in the dark theme on panels and in windows and in the light one on a sunken background.
- The English interface, emails and the installation kit called signing in in different ways; now it is «Sign in» everywhere.
- Sending emails: the mail library is updated with fixes of vulnerabilities in the exchange with the mail server.
- The library that parses web addresses is updated with a fix of a vulnerability.

**Before updating**
- Version 0.9.0-rc.6 cannot be rolled back to 0.9.0-rc.5 with `--rollback`: rc.5 does not work with the database of rc.6. The way back is the copy taken before the update (`sudo ./install.sh --restore`); changes after it are lost. If rc.6 fails to start during the update, the installer restores the copy by itself.
