**Improvements**
- A second factor: an authenticator app with a QR code and recovery codes; passkeys — login by fingerprint, face or PIN without a password; the policy requires a second factor from the administrators of the organization.
- Login through the provider of the organization by OpenID Connect; Keycloak in the delivery for login through Active Directory and LDAP; groups of the provider drive groups of the organization; logging out at the provider ends xtab sessions.
- Profile: photo, name, time zone, a new password and e-mail confirmed by a letter; one's own sessions with ending the others; a letter about a login from a new device.
- Password policy: length and make-up, no reuse, expiry, lockout after wrong attempts, a check against breached passwords.
- The panel of the administrator: members — blocking, administrator rights, ending sessions and tokens, second factor reset, unlocking the login; groups, access with giving oneself a role, policies, SSO login, the permission matrix, organization settings.
- Audit log: filters, CSV export, a chain of seals with signed checkpoints and a daily check, retention with an archive, delivery to SIEM by syslog over TLS; built-in security alerts with letters to the administrators.
- Deleting an account on the request of the person: access stops at once, the data is exported to an archive, the account is anonymized and the values in tables stay; an export of one's own data from the profile.
- Changing the master key and the keys of organizations by commands of the installation administrator.

**Fixes**
- Login with a recovery code: the code itself was not sent.
- Login with a passkey offers the keys saved on the device.
- An answer given before the body of the request is read closes the connection.
