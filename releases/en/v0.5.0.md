**Improvements**
- Automations of a base: an editor with live checks, the triggers «a record is created», «fields changed», «a record began to meet a condition» and «a form was sent», a condition to run, the steps «update a record», «create a record», «send an email» and «HTTP request» with values of fields.
- The run log of automations: the steps and their outcome, running again, «mark as done» for a run with an unknown outcome; an automation that fails again and again is turned off and its creator gets an email.
- Secrets of automations (API keys, passwords) are kept encrypted with the key of the organization and never shown after saving.
- Policies of the administrator: «Outgoing addresses» — where automations may send emails and requests — and the allowed domains of invitations.
- Copies of a table, a base and a workspace: the data of the moment the copy starts, links, files without extra copies, views and forms; the automations of a copied base are turned off and have no secrets.
- Installation on Debian 13; the image is built on Debian 13.

**Fixes**
- The journal of changes and the audit get their partitions for the coming months ahead of time: writing to them does not stop on December 1.
- Excel import: a flaw in reading old `.xls` files (RUSTSEC-2026-0436) is fixed, through which a crafted file could stop the processing of imports.
