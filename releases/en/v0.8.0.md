**Improvements**
- Changing the type of a field with its data: a preview of the result, the move in the background, the old values kept until it is checked.
- Default values and input checks for fields.
- External members: a contractor from another domain can be invited and is marked as external; the policy "Only administrators invite".
- The list of imports with an undo within a day; the period of the run log of automations in the policy of the organization.
- The license of the installation: a 30-day trial, the key installed by an administrator's command, a banner about its term, read-only mode without a valid license.
- Backups: a continuous archive of the database log and nightly copies encrypted with a public key; copies of files; a restore to any moment with one command; a weekly restore drill with its time measured.
- The administration panel of the installation: the license, backups and drills, settings without a restart, background migrations, alerts.
- Moving and upkeep: moving files between storages, an external database, removing the installation, changing the passwords of the services.
- Observability: dashboards and alert rules, a catalogue of metrics, tracing of requests, browser metrics.
- Faster sorting: indexes for frequently used sorts are made automatically.
- Accessibility: a screen reader names the active cell; the grid follows the font size of the browser and a 200 % zoom.
- Closed networks: a build without the internet, a reproducible build, the "About" page with the licenses of components, warnings when the storage or mail is unavailable.
- The memory of the database fits the machine: the PostgreSQL cache is a quarter of the server's memory.
- Confirmed on the customer's stand: 500 concurrent users — reading and writing a record in 15–25 ms, an edit seen by others in 0.15 s (p95); an upgrade under load with about 8 seconds of downtime.

**Fixes**
- Login: successful logins no longer spend the limit of attempts — an office behind one address signs in without refusals.
- The nodes hold the connections of 500 people: the limit of open files is raised.
- The installer checks the bucket of backups in your own S3.
- An Excel sheet with a huge range is refused before it is read.
- After a session ends, the login form is made once and keeps what was typed.
- The state of the license cannot be changed by editing the database past the server.
