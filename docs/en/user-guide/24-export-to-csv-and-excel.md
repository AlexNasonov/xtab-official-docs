Click the down-arrow icon above the table, choose a format — **CSV** or **Excel (XLSX)** — and for CSV a separator, then **Download**. The file gets the records and fields of the view, with its filter and sort. If there are more than 1000 records, or xtab could not count them in time, it asks for your password again.
