In the row at the bottom of the table, click under a column and choose a function: sum, average, minimum, maximum, the number of filled or empty, unique, the earliest or latest date and others. A summary counts the records of the view, with its filter.

When there are very many records and xtab could not count them in time, the number shows an estimate with «≈», or «—» with **«Not counted in time, trying again»**: the exact number appears by itself in a few seconds.
